# Contributing

Thank you for helping maintain the ST-Path Survey resource.

## Before opening a pull request

1. Read the [inclusion and classification criteria](resources/inclusion-criteria.md).
2. Search the repository for an existing entry or alias.
3. Prefer a publisher, DOI, PubMed, arXiv/bioRxiv, official project page, or authors' repository.
4. Label preprints explicitly and update the record when a peer-reviewed version appears.
5. Do not infer a method's category from its title; identify the dominant fusion operation.

## Required information for a method

- method and paper title;
- publication year and status;
- primary paper link;
- official code/model/data link, when available;
- spatial molecular and image modalities;
- dominant fusion mechanism and primary taxonomy category;
- Core, Boundary/Transitional, or Adjacent Support scope label;
- primary downstream task; and
- a short classification rationale.

## Style

- Keep descriptions factual and concise.
- Use en dashes for modality pairs (for example, ST–pathology).
- Use `—` for unavailable resources; do not link to unofficial mirrors without explanation.
- Avoid performance claims unless the dataset, split, metric, and source are named.
- Keep tables alphabetically or chronologically ordered within a defined section.

## Figures

Submit editable vector artwork (`.svg`, `.pdf`, or source format) when available. A raster preview may be included for GitHub rendering. Do not add third-party pathology tiles unless their license and source are documented.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Primary source verified
- [ ] Publication status stated
- [ ] Scope label assigned
- [ ] Dominant mechanism justified
- [ ] Links resolve to official resources
- [ ] No duplicate entry introduced
